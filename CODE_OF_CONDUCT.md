# Code of Conduct

This project follows the principles and enforcement model of
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our standards

We want participation to be safe, constructive, and technically rigorous.
Welcome behavior includes showing empathy across different backgrounds and
experience, giving specific feedback about artifacts rather than attacking
people, acknowledging uncertainty and mistakes, and helping newcomers reproduce
results and understand project conventions.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
humiliation, doxxing, sustained disruption, and publishing another person's
private information are not acceptable. Technical disagreement does not excuse
personal abuse.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, Discussions, reviews,
project events, and private communication undertaken as a representative of
CGRX.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct incidents privately using the GitHub private reporting route
documented in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not place sensitive evidence in a
public issue. The maintainer will acknowledge the report, protect the reporter's
privacy as far as reasonably possible, investigate conflicts of interest, and
communicate the outcome to affected parties.

Responses are proportional to severity and pattern: clarification or correction,
formal warning, temporary participation restriction, or permanent removal from
project spaces. A maintainer involved in an incident must not decide that report
alone; another trusted reviewer will be asked to handle it.
